  secret scanner in CI.

  `ExternalProjectRealityReport`, and `ProjectCapabilityProfile` contracts.

  `c67ff5e28205aac57ff28e8b8120c3bac5de4488`.

- No Supabase connection.
- No database write.
- No production mutation.
